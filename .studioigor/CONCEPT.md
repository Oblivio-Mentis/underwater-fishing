# Concept - Underwater Fishing

[user] First-person cooperative incremental fishing on a dark deep-sea expedition, 2–5 players.

- Genre: [user] cooperative fishing and exploration with incremental progression and encounters with marine predators.
- References: [user] The Long Dark silhouettes, ABZU light, DREDGE mood, SOMA interiors.
- Fantasy: [user] submarine crew sharing catches and progression.
- 30-sec loop: [user] harpoon, tension struggle, capture, carry.
- 5-15 min loop: [user] dive, hoist, feed/process, upgrade.
- Session loop: [user] permanent technology and access to new depths.
- Camera/controls: [user] first person, mouse/keyboard, Input Actions.
- Modes/platforms: [user] Windows x64 listen co-op2-5; debug solo; Steam now.
- Story: [user] research ship attacked by hungry ancient creature; no extra lore/cutscenes.
- Failure: [user] bounded unprocessed-stock loss; permanent upgrades preserved.
- Session duration: [open] tune from playtest.
- Scale: [user] two greybox depth configurations; stages A/B/C from Docs/BRIEF.md.
## Pillars
- Server confirms interactions and single consumption.
- Upgrades increase actual extraction efficiency.
- Teammates help fishing and rescue.
- Local failure preserves technology.
## Anti-pillars
- No money, quota/reset extraction, village, idle factory, elaborate graphics or cutscenes.
